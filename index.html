EarBridge

Hear your surroundings in your good ear.
Which ear can't hear?
Volume boost: +12 dB
Above +18 dB adds hiss. Moving the phone closer to the speaker sounds better than a huge boost.
Noise cleanup: 6 / 10
Learns the steady background noise (fans, traffic, hiss) and removes it from the sound, even while people talk. Higher is stronger. Too high can make voices sound thin. 0 turns it off.
Find the voice (works on any phone)
Hold the phone upright at your chest and turn slowly in a full circle while the person talks. Your body blocks a little sound from behind, so the app finds the loudest direction. Then the arrow points to the voice and you hear two quick ticks when you face it.

Deaf-side alert (experimental)
Start listening, then tap Calibrate and clap or talk on your deaf side.
Microphone
Pick the iPhone microphone, not your earbuds. Earbud mics make sound worse.
Keep this screen open while listening. Put the phone mic toward the people you want to hear. If a call or alert interrupts, EarBridge resumes by itself. If it cannot, tap the orange button. For music, calls and videos: Settings > Accessibility > Audio & Visual, turn on Mono Audio and slide Balance to your good ear. Start with low volume and raise it slowly.
