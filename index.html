Which ear can't hear?

+12 dB

Above +18 dB adds hiss. Moving the phone closer to the speaker sounds better than a huge boost.

5 / 10

Learns steady background noise (fans, traffic, hiss) and turns it down, even while people talk. Too high can make voices sound thin. 0 turns it off.

Find the voice

Hold the phone upright at your chest and turn slowly in a full circle while the person talks. The arrow on the dial then points to the voice, and you hear two quick ticks when you face it.

Deaf-side alert (experimental)

Start listening, then tap Calibrate and clap or talk on your deaf side.

Microphone and filter

Pick the iPhone microphone, not your earbuds. Earbud mics make sound worse.

Good to know

Keep this screen open while listening and point the phone mic toward the people you want to hear.

If a call or alert interrupts, EarBridge resumes by itself. If it cannot, tap the orange button.

For music, calls and videos: Settings > Accessibility > Audio & Visual, turn on Mono Audio and slide Balance to your good ear.

Start with low volume and raise it slowly. Your settings are remembered.
